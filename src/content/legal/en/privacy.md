## This website {#website}

This website is a static information page without sign-in, a contact form or a newsletter. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in localStorage, and includes no analytics or tracking services, no external fonts, no CDNs and no embedded media. It collects no calculation, customer or business data. The website creates no usage or advertising profiles and makes no automated decision, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR applies to necessary contract-related requests, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR with the legitimate interest in replying. Sending is not consent. The website is provided through GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. GitHub, Inc. is the recipient of the server logs, including IP addresses, and processes them for delivery and security. This can involve a transfer to the United States. That transfer relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. RevenueCat is not integrated on this website. Content from other providers is not loaded here. If you open an external link, the destination rules apply.

We do not operate a visitor database on this website. GitHub, Inc. stores the server logs, including IP addresses, for the operation and security of GitHub Pages. We keep support messages for as long as we need them to handle the request, and longer only where a statutory retention duty requires it.

## The OfflineHandwerk app {#app}

The app runs on Android and iOS. Purchase details apply only when the mobile store integration is active. Calculations, customer and business data, contact details, line items, internal notes, employee and catalog records, business profile, logo and settings are stored in the local SQLite database on your device. We do not collect these contents, transfer them to our servers or store them there. There is no sign-in with us and no synchronization of these contents through our servers.

Your business is responsible for the lawful processing of the customer and employee data you enter, including its legal basis, information duties, access rights and retention. We cannot access those local records. For them, we are neither the controller nor a processor. This does not apply to messages you send us or to purchase and license data.

When you choose an image as your business logo, the app uses only the photo you select from your library and stores it locally for your PDFs. Camera and microphone access are not enabled in the app configuration. You import a backup only by selecting a file yourself. The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. We do not track your calculations or work steps and do not create usage or advertising profiles. There is no automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

The mobile store integration initializes RevenueCat with an API key only, without a custom app user ID and without an email attribute. RevenueCat generates an App User ID. This identifier is pseudonymous, not guaranteed to be anonymous. RevenueCat receives purchase, transaction and entitlement data and the technical data the SDK needs to unlock and restore purchases. The app sends neither an email address nor calculation, customer or business contents to RevenueCat. The store integration stores the last known plan locally on the device. Purchases run through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google process the purchase and transaction data under their own privacy notices. The check can also run for Free users and without an active subscription. It is not a sign-in. There is no email sign-in, no sign-in counter and no email-to-license mapping on an OfflineHandwerk server.

The purpose of license verification is to provide purchased features. Where this processing is necessary for that purpose, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Purchased features cannot be unlocked without this check. An app without the store integration does not use this processing. Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR does not cover advertising profiles; we do not create such profiles. When the store integration is active, Apple or Google receive the purchase data and RevenueCat, Inc. in the United States receives the license data. License data may be processed in the United States. The published RevenueCat terms provide EU Standard Contractual Clauses for this transfer.

When you choose Share, Print or email, the app, mail provider or printer you select receives the locally created PDF. We do not relay this file through our servers. An email to your office or to a customer address attaches the PDF, in the mail you start, to that address. The problem-reporting feature opens your email app with a message to info@offlinehandwerk.de. The message contains the app version, build, operating system, device model, plan and your description. The app does not send it itself and does not attach calculation or customer data or store identifiers. You review and send the message yourself. If you send the email, we receive your sender address, the message and files you attach yourself, so that we can handle your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for necessary contract-related requests, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR with the legitimate interest in answering requests and fixing faults. Sending an email is not consent. Support is optional. Without a reachable contact address we cannot reply.

Local records remain until you delete them or reset the app. Resetting deletes the local database. Archiving is not deletion. Normal uninstallation removes the local database. Operating-system backups may keep copies. We cannot remotely retrieve or delete the local database or recover lost local contents. PDFs and JSON backups are created locally. The JSON backup is unencrypted and stays on the device until you choose a destination in the share dialog. The app does not upload backups. Exported files remain at the chosen destination until you delete them there. Resetting the app does not delete those copies, support emails, or purchase data held by Apple, Google or RevenueCat. Purchase and entitlement data are needed while purchased features are provided and purchases can be restored. Apple, Google and RevenueCat determine retention of the transaction data they store themselves. We keep support messages for as long as we need them to handle the request, and longer only where a statutory retention duty requires it.

Local use requires no registration and no transmission of your work data to us. A reply to support requires a reachable sender address. Purchased features cannot be unlocked without the license check.
